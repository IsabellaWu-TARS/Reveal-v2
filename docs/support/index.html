Reveal — Support

One verse a day, one reflection, one small action.

Getting started

Reveal shows the same card to everyone on a given day. It changes at midnight, and it stays put if you close and reopen the app.

  • Flip the card — tap it. The front is the verse, the back is the reflection and the action.
  • Favourite — the star in the top-right corner of the card.
  • Write a note — tap the note area under the card while the card is flipped.
  • Read the full chapter — open the Scripture page and tap the reference.
  • Look back — the Calendar page shows every day, and opens any day you have already seen, favourited or noted.
  • Daily reminder — Settings → Daily Reminders, then pick a time.
  • Card text colour — Settings → Card Text Colour. Automatic, White, Ink, or any colour you like.

Common questions

The verse text is hard to read

The default ink is white. On the paler cards that is genuinely low contrast — this is a trade-off we have left to you rather than force. Open Settings → Card Text Colour → Automatic and the app picks the most legible ink for each card.

My reminder never arrives

  • Settings → Daily Reminders must be on, with a time set.
  • iOS Settings → Notifications → Reveal must allow notifications, with a sound and no Focus mode blocking it.
  • The reminder repeats daily, so it fires at the next occurrence of the time you picked — not immediately.

The full chapter did not load

English chapter text is fetched from a public Bible API and needs a connection; Chinese chapters are built into the app and read with no network at all. The day's verse is always stored on your device and stays readable offline; when a request fails the chapter area shows a message and a Retry button.

Where does the Bible text come from?

English: the World English Bible (public domain), with chapter text requested live from bible.helloao.org. Chinese: the Chinese Union Version, 1919 edition, bundled inside the app and never sent over the network. Attribution details are in the app under Settings → About Bible Sources.

How do I delete my data?

Notes, favourites, reading history and settings live only on your device. Clear a note in the note editor, tap the star again to remove a favourite, or delete the app to remove everything at once. Nothing is stored on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Does Reveal collect anything?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

开始使用

在同一天里，所有人看到的都是同一张卡片。卡片在午夜更换；关闭 App 再打开，卡片不会变。

  • 翻卡 —— 轻点卡片。正面是经文，背面是反思与行动。
  • 收藏 —— 卡片右上角的星标。
  • 写笔记 —— 在翻到背面时，点卡片下方的笔记区域。
  • 阅读全章 —— 进入经文页，点击出处。
  • 回看 —— 日历页可以看到每一天；已经看过、收藏或写过笔记的日期都能打开。
  • 每日提醒 —— 设置 → 每日提醒，然后选择时间。
  • 卡片文字颜色 —— 设置 → 卡片文字颜色。可选自动、白色、墨色，或任意颜色。

常见问题

经文太浅，看不清

默认文字颜色为白色。在颜色较浅的卡片上，对比度确实很低——这一点我们交给你决定，而不是替你锁定。打开 设置 → 卡片文字颜色 → 自动，App 会为每张卡片自动挑选最易读的颜色。

提醒没有出现

  • 设置 → 每日提醒需要开启，并设定时间。
  • iOS 设置 → 通知 → Reveal 需要允许通知、允许声音，且未被专注模式拦截。
  • 提醒是每天重复的，会在你设定时间的下一次到来时触发，而不是立刻触发。

全章经文加载不出来

英文全章来自公开的圣经 API，需要联网；中文全章已内置在 App 内，读取时完全不联网。当天的经文始终保存在本机，离线也能阅读；请求失败时全章区域会显示提示与「重试」按钮。

经文来源

英文：World English Bible（公有领域），全章内容实时取自 bible.helloao.org。中文：官话和合本（1919 年版），全本内置在 App 内，不经过网络。版权说明可在 App 内「设置 → 资料来源与版权声明」查看。

如何删除我的数据

笔记、收藏、阅读记录与设置都只保存在你的设备上。清空笔记、再次点击星标取消收藏，或直接删除 App 即可一次清除全部内容。我们没有任何服务器副本。

Reveal 会收集数据吗

不会。没有账号、没有分析统计、没有广告、没有第三方 SDK。详见隐私政策。
