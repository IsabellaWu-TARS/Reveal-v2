Privacy Policy

Reveal — a daily Bible verse, a daily reflection, a daily action.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Short version

Reveal has no servers and no accounts. Your notes, favourites and preferences are stored only on your iPhone and are never transmitted to us.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The only network request the app makes is to download Bible text from a public, non-personal Bible API when you tap to read a full chapter.

1. Who we are

Reveal ("the App") is a devotional app published by Reveal Mashal. This policy explains what information the App handles, what we do with it, and the choices you have. Questions about this policy can be sent to revealmashal@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Reveal is built around on-device storage. The following items are written to your iPhone's app sandbox (via iOS UserDefaults) and never leave the device:

WhatWhy
Reflection notes you write for a verseSo your personal reflections are preserved for you
Verse IDs you mark as favouritesSo you can revisit the verses you want to keep
Dates on which you opened a cardSo the calendar can show your reading history
Language and appearance preferencesSo the App looks and reads the way you chose
Daily reminder on/off and reminder timeSo we schedule the notification you asked for

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, back it up, restore it, or hand it over to anyone. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Data we do not collect

Reveal does not collect, transmit or sell:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or user account of any kind
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no identifier-for-advertising tracking
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking; App Tracking Transparency is therefore not used
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution SDKs
  • No precise or coarse location, no contacts, no photos, no camera, no microphone
  • No health, fitness or financial information
  • No purchases or payment data

4. Network requests the App makes

When you tap a verse reference to read the full chapter, the App fetches that chapter over HTTPS from a public Bible text API (bible.helloao.org). This request contains no personal data, no device identifier and no advertising identifier — only the requested book and chapter number. We do not operate this API and do not receive request logs tied to you.

Apple's Push Notification service is used solely to deliver the daily reminder, and only if you turn it on. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and makes no other outbound requests.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhyRequired?
NotificationsTo deliver the optional daily verse reminder at the time you choseNo — fully optional

You can decline notifications and still use every feature of the App. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Daily Reminders, or in the iOS Settings app.

6. Third-party services

Reveal does not use a backend of its own. The only third party involved in delivering content is the public Bible text API mentioned in section 4, which receives no personal data. Apple provides iCloud device backup services and Push Notification delivery under Apple's own privacy terms; Apple's standard data handling applies to any device backup the user chooses to enable.

7. Children's privacy

Reveal is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the App itself. Because the App has no accounts and transmits no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a shared device, please contact us and we will help you clear it by deleting the App.

8. Your rights and controls

Because your data stays on your device, you are always in control of it:

  • Delete a note — open the note editor, clear the text; it is saved automatically as you type. Or delete the App.
  • Delete a favourite — tap the star again to remove it. Or delete the App.
  • Delete everything — delete the App from your device. All notes, favourites, history and settings are removed.
  • Stop the reminder — turn off Daily Reminders in Settings, or in iOS Settings.

Reveal does not hold a copy of your personal data, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on your behalf — and nothing for us to disclose.

9. Security

Reveal uses iOS platform encryption for data at rest and Apple's standard protections for the App sandbox. Data is never transmitted to servers operated by us. We follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines for permission prompts, so the App asks only when the feature is actually used.

10. Your data and iCloud backup

If you have iCloud backup enabled on your device, your notes and favourites may be included in your device backup under Apple's iCloud privacy terms. That backup is controlled by you via your Apple Account, and Reveal has no access to it.

11. International transfers

Reveal does not transfer personal data to servers in other countries, because personal data is not transmitted to us in the first place.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, describe it in the App's release notes. Continuing to use Reveal after a change means the updated policy applies.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding privacy, contact us at revealmashal@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.

4. App 的联网行为

当你点击经文出处以阅读全章时，App 会通过 HTTPS 从一个公开的圣经文本 API（bible.helloao.org）获取该章内容。此请求不包含任何个人数据、设备标识符或广告标识符，只有被请求的书名与章节号。该 API 并非由我们运营，我们也无法获得与你关联的请求日志。

Apple 的推送通知服务仅用于发送每日提醒，且仅在你开启后启用。我们不会将其用于任何营销或广告用途。

本 App 不含任何第三方广告或分析 SDK，也不发起其他对外请求。

8. 你的权利与控制

因为数据留在你的设备上，你始终掌握它：

  • 取消收藏 —— 再次点击星标即可移除。也可以删除 App。
  • 删除全部数据 —— 从设备删除本 App，所有笔记、收藏、记录与设置一并清除。
  • 关闭提醒 —— 在设置中关闭每日提醒，或在 iOS 系统设置中关闭通知。

Reveal 手中不保留你的个人数据副本，因此我们无法代为导出、更正或删除 —— 也不存在需要向我们披露的内容。

9. 安全

Reveal 使用 iOS 平台的静态加密机制保护本地数据，并依赖 Apple 对 App 沙盒的标准保护。数据从不传输至我们运营的服务器。权限提示遵循 Apple 人机界面指南，仅在用户实际使用相关功能时才请求。
