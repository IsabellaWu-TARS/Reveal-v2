### **Hebrews**

* For the word of God is living and active, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the dividing of soul and spirit, of both joints and marrow, and is able to discern the thoughts and intentions of the heart. There is no creature that is hidden from his sight, but all things are naked and laid open before the eyes of him to whom we must give an account. (Hebrews 4:12–13, WEB)  
- God's word pierces and discerns every thought and intention. Do you let it expose your heart fully, or do you try to hide from the One to whom you must give account?  
* For we don’t have a high priest who can’t be touched with the feeling of our infirmities, but one who has been in all points tempted like we are, yet without sin. Let’s therefore draw near with boldness to the throne of grace, that we may receive mercy and may find grace for help in time of need. (Hebrews 4:15–16, WEB)  
- Your High Priest sympathizes with your weaknesses and invites bold access. Do you draw near confidently for mercy and grace, or stay distant in shame?  
* For by one offering he has perfected forever those who are being sanctified. (Hebrews 10:14, WEB)  
- By one offering He perfected you forever. Are you resting in this completed perfection while being sanctified, or do you keep striving to perfect yourself?  
* Now faith is assurance of things hoped for, proof of things not seen. (Hebrews 11:1, WEB)  
- Faith is assurance and conviction of the unseen. Does your faith provide real proof and certainty of hope, or is it still vague and wavering?  
* Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever. (Hebrews 13:8, WEB)  
- Jesus is unchanging. Do you anchor your life in His eternal sameness, or let shifting feelings and circumstances unsettle your trust?

### **James**

* But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach, and it will be given to him. (James 1:5, WEB)  
- If you lack wisdom, ask God who gives generously without reproach. Do you ask boldly and expectantly, or do you hesitate fearing criticism?  
* Blessed is a person who endures temptation, for when he has been approved, he will receive the crown of life which the Lord promised to those who love him. (James 1:12, WEB)  
- Enduring temptation brings the crown of life. Do you endure trials with love for Him, or do you cave quickly and miss the promised reward?  
* So, then, my beloved brothers, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to anger; for the anger of man doesn’t produce the righteousness of God. (James 1:19–20, WEB)  
- Be quick to hear, slow to speak and anger. Does your anger produce God's righteousness, or does human anger still dominate and destroy?  
* But be doers of the word, and not only hearers, deluding your own selves. (James 1:22, WEB)  
- Be doers not just hearers. Do you delude yourself by listening without obeying, or does the word actually change your actions?  
* Even so faith, if it has no works, is dead in itself. (James 2:17, WEB)  
- Faith without works is dead. Does your faith show life through deeds, or is it a lifeless profession without fruit?  
* Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show by his good conduct that his deeds are done in gentleness of wisdom. But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your heart, don’t boast and don’t lie against the truth. This wisdom is not that which comes down from above, but is earthly, sensual, and demonic. For where jealousy and selfish ambition are, there is confusion and every evil deed. But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceful, gentle, reasonable, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. Now the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace. (James 3:13–18, WEB)  
- True wisdom from above is pure, peaceable, gentle, merciful. Does your conduct reveal heavenly wisdom, or does jealousy and ambition produce confusion and evil?  
* But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, “God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.” Be subject therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. (James 4:6–8, WEB)  
- God gives more grace to the humble. Do you submit to Him, resist the devil, and draw near so he flees and God comes close, or does pride keep you distant?  
* But above all things, my brothers, don’t swear—not by heaven, or by the earth, or by any other oath; but let your “yes” be “yes”, and your “no”, “no”, so that you don’t fall into hypocrisy. (James 5:12, WEB)  
- Let your yes be yes and no be no. Do your words carry integrity without oaths, or does exaggeration and hypocrisy still mark your speech?  
* Confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The insistent prayer of a righteous person is powerfully effective. (James 5:16, WEB)  
- Confess sins to one another and pray for healing. Do you practice honest confession and fervent prayer, or do you hide sin and pray weakly?

### **1 Peter**

* Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to his great mercy caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead. (1 Peter 1:3, WEB)  
- Born again to living hope through Christ's resurrection. Do you live in the reality of this new birth and hope, or has the old life still held on?

### **1 John**

* This is the message which we have heard from him and announce to you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all. (1 John 1:5, WEB)  
- God is light with no darkness. Do you walk in His light without compromise, or do hidden shadows still linger in your life?  
* Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another. (1 John 4:11, WEB)  
- If God loved us this way, we ought to love one another. Does His love compel you to love others sacrificially, or do you withhold it when it costs?

### **2 John**

* Whoever transgresses and doesn’t remain in the teaching of Christ, doesn’t have God. He who remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son. (2 John 1:9, WEB)  
- Abide in Christ's teaching to have both Father and Son. Do you remain steadfast in His doctrine, or do you wander and risk losing fellowship with God?

### **3 John**

* Beloved, you do a faithful work in whatever you accomplish for those who are brothers and strangers. (3 John 1:5, WEB)  
- You do faithful work for brothers and strangers. Do you show hospitality and support to all believers, or is your care limited to those you know?  
* Beloved, don’t imitate that which is evil, but that which is good. He who does good is of God. He who does evil hasn’t seen God. (3 John 1:11, WEB)  
- Imitate good not evil. Does your life show you have seen God through doing good, or does evil imitation reveal you haven't truly seen Him?

### **Jude**

* But you, beloved, keep building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit. Keep yourselves in God’s love, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ to eternal life. (Jude 1:20–21, WEB)  
- Build yourself up in faith, pray in the Spirit, stay in God's love, look for mercy. Do you actively maintain this life of faith and anticipation, or do you neglect the foundations?

### **Revelation**

* Behold, he is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, including those who pierced him. All the tribes of the earth will mourn over him. Even so, Amen. (Revelation 1:7, WEB)  
- He is coming and every eye will see Him. Does the certainty of His return fill you with anticipation and readiness, or does it leave you unmoved and unprepared?
