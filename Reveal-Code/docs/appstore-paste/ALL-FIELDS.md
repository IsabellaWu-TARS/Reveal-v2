## Description — English

Reveal gives you one verse a day — and a moment to sit with it.

Each morning a single card appears: a verse on the front, a reflection and one
small action on the back. Tap to turn it over. That is the whole ritual, and it
takes about a minute.

A CARD THAT CHANGES ONCE A DAY
The card is tied to the date, not to when you open the app. Reopen it a dozen
times before midnight and it will still be the same verse. At midnight it turns
over to the next one, and keeps a record of every day behind you.

314 DEVOTIONALS
Each card pairs the verse with a reflection question and one small action.
314 cards covering 55 books, in English and Simplified Chinese.

REMEMBER WHAT MOVED YOU
Star a card to keep it. Write a note while you sit with it. The calendar remembers
both, and every day you opened the app: tap any past day to see the verse you had,
what you wrote, and what you were thinking that week.

READ AROUND IT
Tap any reference to read the whole chapter, with the day's verse highlighted in
place. The Chinese text is stored on your device in full, so it reads fine on a
plane; the English chapter is fetched when you ask for it.

SHARE THE CARD, NOT A SCREENSHOT
Share the card as it looks — verse side, reflection side, or both — as a clean
image with your note as text. No watermarks, no sign-up to view it.

QUIET BY DESIGN
· No account. No sign-in. Nothing to create.
· No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
· Your notes stay on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.
· One optional daily reminder at a time you choose.
· Light, dark, and a card text colour you can set yourself.

TWO LANGUAGES
English (World English Bible, public domain) and 简体中文 (Chinese Union Version of
1919, public domain). Switch at any time in Settings — the card, the reflection and
the reminder all follow. Chinese readers can also read the 1919 text in traditional
characters, exactly as printed, with one switch in Settings.

WHAT IT IS NOT
No streaks to maintain. No badges. No feed. Nothing to catch up on if you miss a
week. Open it when you want a quiet minute.

## Description — 简体中文

每天一节经文，留一点时间给自己。

每天早上，一张卡片出现：正面是经文，背面是一段反思和一个可以立刻去做的小行动。轻点翻面，整个过程大约一分钟。

每天只翻一次
卡片与日期绑定，而不是与你打开 App 的次数绑定。一天之内反复打开，看到的仍是同一节经文；午夜之后，卡片会翻到下一张，同时把过去的每一天都记下来。

314 张卡片
每张卡片都配有经文、一段反思和一个行动。共 314 张，涵盖 55 卷书，中英双语。

留住打动你的
点亮星标即可收藏，坐下来时可以写下笔记。日历会记住这两者，也会记住你打开过 App 的每一天——点开任何过去的日期，都能看到当天的经文、你写下的文字。

读回上下文
点按任何经文出处，即可阅读整章，当天的经文会在原位高亮。中文全本经文已保存在手机里，飞行模式下也能读；英文整章在需要时才联网获取。

分享的是卡片，不是截图
可以把经文面、反思面或两面一起，以干净的图片分享出去，笔记则以文字分享。没有水印，对方也不需要注册。

安静的设计
· 没有账号，不需要登录。
· 没有分析统计、没有广告、没有追踪、没有第三方 SDK。
· 笔记只保存在你的 iPhone 上，从不上传。
· 每日提醒可选，时间由你设定。
· 支持浅色、深色，卡片文字颜色也可以自行设定。

两种语言
中文（官话和合本，1919 年版，公有领域）与英文（World English Bible，公有领域）。随时在设置中切换——卡片、反思和提醒都会跟着改变。中文还可在设置中切换简体/繁体字形，1919 年原样的标点与用字保持不变。

它不是
没有需要维持的连续打卡，没有徽章，没有信息流。错过一周，也不会欠下什么。想安静一分钟的时候，打开它就好。
